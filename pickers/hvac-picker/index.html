Heat pump vs. straight cool

Both cool the same in summer. The difference is winter heat.

★ Sharma recommends for most Florida homes

Heat pump

  • Cools AND heats — one system does both jobs.
  • In summer it pulls heat out of your home. In winter it runs in reverse and pulls heat in from the outdoor air.
  • Because it moves heat instead of making it, it heats much more efficiently — ideal for Florida's mild winters.
  • Electric heat strips inside are only backup, for the coldest mornings.
  • Every system in this picker — Goodman, Carrier and the ductless mini-split — is a heat pump.
Cooling only

Straight cool

  • Cools only — the outdoor unit cannot heat your home at all.
  • For winter heat it relies entirely on electric heat strips inside the air handler (or a separate furnace, if you have one).
  • Heat strips make heat directly from electricity, so they cost more to run whenever it's cold.
  • Simple and common in older Florida homes — but you pay for every bit of winter heat at heat-strip rates.

Bottom line: If you have a straight cool now, a heat pump change-out gives you the same cooling plus far cheaper winter heat. No ducts in the room at all? A mini-split is a heat pump too — same idea, ductless. Not sure what you have now? Tell us in your notes — Sharma will check what's there and quote the right replacement.
